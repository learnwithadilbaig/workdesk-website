Attendance FAQ

Attendance and leave questions

Do we need new attendance hardware?

No. Staff can check in from the WorkDesk app, and if you already use a biometric or card machine you can import its attendance file.

Can we have different shifts for different teams?

Yes. Each team, branch or person can have its own shift pattern, including rotating and night shifts.

How are leave balances calculated?

You set accrual, carry-forward and maximum balance rules for each leave type. WorkDesk applies them automatically and shows each employee their balance in the app.

Can managers approve leave from their phone?

Yes. Requests arrive as notifications and can be approved or declined in one tap, with an optional comment.
