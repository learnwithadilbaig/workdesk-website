Security

How we look after your people's data

Salaries, CNIC numbers and bank details are some of the most sensitive records a company keeps. Here is how WorkDesk protects them, and what you control yourself.

{{icon:lock}}

Access by role

You decide what owners, HR, managers and employees can see and change. Salaries are visible only to roles you allow.

{{icon:globe}}

Encrypted connections

Everything between your browser or phone and WorkDesk travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

{{icon:history}}

Activity history

Changes to salaries, policies and employee records are logged with who made them and when.

{{icon:cloud}}

Backups

Data is backed up regularly so it can be restored if something goes wrong.

{{icon:key}}

Account protection

Strong password rules and the ability to remove access the moment someone leaves.

{{icon:download}}

Your data stays yours

Export your records whenever you want. We never sell your data or your employees' data.

What you can do to keep data safe

  • Give each person the smallest role that lets them do their job.
  • Remove access on an employee's last working day. Marking someone as left does this automatically.
  • Use a unique password for WorkDesk and don't share accounts.
  • Review the activity history for salary changes before approving payroll.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security problem in WorkDesk, please email {{cfg:contact_email}} with the details. We'll acknowledge your report and keep you updated while we investigate.
