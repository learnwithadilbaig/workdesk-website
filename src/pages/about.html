About WorkDesk

We build software for the people who keep companies running

HR managers, accountants, shift supervisors and owners hold growing businesses together, usually with spreadsheets, a biometric machine and a lot of WhatsApp. WorkDesk is the system we think they deserve: one place for people, attendance, payroll and work.

Why two ticks

The best moment in HR is when something gets done

Our logo is two check marks that overlap to form a W. In Pakistan, two ticks are what you see when a message has been delivered. For us they mean a request that didn't get lost: the leave approved, the salary paid, the task closed.

Everything we build is judged by one question. Does it get more things to that second tick, with less chasing?

What we believe

A few things we won't compromise on

Rules beat favours

A written policy applied the same way to everyone is fairer than a manager deciding case by case. Our software makes the fair way the easy way.

Employees deserve to see their own data

If money is deducted from your salary, you should be able to see exactly why. Transparency settles most HR disputes before they start.

Local details matter

Month-end payroll, shift work, multiple branches, CNIC records and Urdu names spelled three ways. We build for how work actually happens here.

Your data is yours

You can export your records at any time. We earn your business every month instead of locking you in.

How we work with customers

We set it up with you, not for you

Every new customer gets help moving their data and writing their policies into WorkDesk. We sit in on the first payroll run. After that, support is a message away, from people who understand HR and payroll.
