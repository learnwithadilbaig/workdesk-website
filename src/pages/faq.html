Frequently asked questions

The questions businesses ask before they choose WorkDesk. If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

The basics

What is WorkDesk?

WorkDesk is HR, payroll and work management software for growing businesses in Pakistan. It keeps employee records, attendance and shifts, leave, payroll, tasks and projects, performance scorecards and reports in one system.

What size of business is WorkDesk for?

Most customers have between 20 and a few thousand employees. If you're past the point where one person can keep HR in their head, WorkDesk will help.

Do we have to use every module?

No. Choose the modules you need and add others later. Many companies start with attendance and payroll.

Payroll

Can payroll include fines, advances and loans?

Yes. Define fines, advances and loan instalments once and they're applied in each payroll run, with every line explained on the payslip. See payroll software.

Can we add income tax, provident fund and EOBI?

Yes, as payroll components. Because rates change, confirm current figures with your tax adviser and update them when the budget changes.

How do employees get their payslips?

In the mobile app and on the web. They can also download a PDF.

Attendance and leave

How do employees check in?

From the WorkDesk mobile app or the web. If you use a biometric or card machine, you can import its attendance file.

Can we have shifts and multiple branches?

Yes. Each branch, team or person can have its own shift pattern, timings and holiday calendar.

Can leave policies differ by employee type?

Yes. Set different entitlements for permanent staff, contract staff and people on probation.

Data and security

Who can see salaries?

Only the roles you allow. Access is set by role, so managers can see their team's attendance and tasks without seeing salaries.

Can we export our data?

Yes, at any time. Your data belongs to you. Read more on our security page.

Getting started and pricing

How long does set-up take?

Most teams are ready for their first payroll within two to four weeks, including data import and a parallel payroll month. Our guide on moving from Excel explains each step.

How is WorkDesk priced?

Per active employee per month, for the modules you choose. Build your plan on the pricing page and we'll send a written quote.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Book a demo and we'll set up a trial with your own policies.
