Tasks FAQ

Task and project questions

Is this a replacement for Trello or Asana?

For most HR and operations work, yes. Teams that run large software projects may still prefer a dedicated tool. WorkDesk's strength is that tasks sit next to attendance, leave and people data.

Can employees create their own tasks?

Yes. Anyone can create tasks for themselves, and managers can create tasks for their team.

Do tasks show on the mobile app?

Yes. Staff see what's due today, update progress and mark tasks done from their phones.
