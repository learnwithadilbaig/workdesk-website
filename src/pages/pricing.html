Pricing FAQ

Questions about pricing

What counts as an active employee?

Anyone on your payroll or attendance during the month. People who left before the month started aren't counted.

Is there a free trial?

You can sign up and explore WorkDesk with sample data, or book a demo and we'll set up a trial with your own policies.

Can we start small and add modules later?

Yes. Many teams start with attendance and payroll, then add tasks and scorecards once those are running smoothly.

Do you offer annual billing?

Ask us when you request your quote. We'll show monthly and annual options side by side.
