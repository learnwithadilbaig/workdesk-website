Getting started

What should I set up first?

In this order: company details and branches, departments, shifts and working hours, leave types, then employees. Payroll components come last, once people are in the system.

How do I invite my managers?

Give each manager the Manager role on their employee profile. They'll receive an invitation to sign in and will see only their own team.

Employees

How do I import employees from a spreadsheet?

Download the import template, paste your data into it with one row per employee, and upload it. WorkDesk shows any rows with problems before importing so you can fix them first. Our onboarding team can do this with you.

How do I archive someone who has left?

Open their profile, choose Mark as left, and enter the last working day. Their history stays available for letters and audits, and they stop counting as an active employee from the next month.

Attendance and leave

How do I set a grace period for late arrivals?

Open the shift, set the start time and the grace period in minutes. Check-ins within the grace period are not marked late.

How do I change an employee's leave balance?

Open the employee's leave tab and add an adjustment with a reason. The reason is saved so the change is clear later.

Payroll

How do I add an allowance or deduction?

Create it as a payroll component, choose whether it's an earning or deduction, fixed or calculated, then add it to the right salary structures or individual employees.

Can I preview payroll before approving it?

Yes. Every run starts as a draft. Review totals and individual payslips, fix anything that looks wrong, then approve.

Mobile app

How do employees sign in to the app?

Employees receive an invitation when their profile is created. They set a password from the link and then sign in on the app with their work email or phone number.

Someone can't check in from the app. What should I check?

Make sure they have a shift assigned for today and that their app is up to date. If it still doesn't work, contact support with their employee ID.

Account and billing

How do I add a module?

Contact your account manager or our support team. New modules use your existing employee data, so they're usually ready the same day.

Where can I find invoices?

Owners and account admins can download invoices from Settings, under Billing.
