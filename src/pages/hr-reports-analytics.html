Reports FAQ

Reporting questions

Can we build our own reports?

Yes. Start from a ready-made report, choose the columns and filters you need, and save it for next month.

Which formats can we export?

Excel and PDF. Excel keeps the raw numbers for your finance team; PDF is ready to share with management.

Can managers see reports for their own team only?

Yes. Reports follow the same role-based access as the rest of WorkDesk, so managers see their team and owners see everything.
