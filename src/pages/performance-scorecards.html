Scorecards FAQ

Performance questions

Can we use our existing appraisal form?

Yes. Recreate your current criteria and rating scale as a scorecard template, then improve it over time.

Do we have to use numbers for every goal?

No. Mix measurable KPIs with rated goals such as teamwork or customer handling, each with its own weight.

Can employees see their own scorecard?

Yes, and that's the point. People who can see their progress during the quarter rarely argue about the result at the end.
