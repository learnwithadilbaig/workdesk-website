Fines, handled fairly

Fines that follow your policy, not someone's mood

Late fines cause more arguments than any other line on a payslip. The fix is boring and it works: write the rule down, apply it to everyone the same way, and show people exactly why money was deducted.

  • {{tick}}
    Grace periods and thresholdsFor example, ten minutes of grace, and a deduction only after the third late arrival in a month.
  • {{tick}}
    Visible to the employeeStaff can see each late check-in that counted toward a fine before payroll is closed.
  • {{tick}}
    Exceptions with a reasonManagers can waive a fine, and the reason stays on record for audit.

Payroll FAQ

Payroll questions

Can WorkDesk handle income tax on salaries in Pakistan?

You can add income tax withholding as a component of each employee's payroll. Tax slabs change with the federal budget, so confirm current rates with your tax adviser and update them in WorkDesk when they change.

Can different employees have different salary structures?

Yes. Create structures for each grade or role, then override individual allowances where someone's package is different.

What happens if attendance was wrong after payroll is closed?

Correct the record and the difference is carried into the next payroll run as an adjustment, shown on its own line so the employee knows what happened.

Do employees see how fines were calculated?

Yes. Each fine on a payslip links back to the attendance entries that caused it, which takes most of the heat out of payroll day.
