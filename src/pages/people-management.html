People FAQ

Employee records questions

Can we import our existing employee list?

Yes. Upload your current spreadsheet and WorkDesk maps the columns to employee fields. We help with the first import so nothing is lost.

Who can see salary information?

Only roles you allow. By default HR and owners see salaries, managers see their own team's attendance and tasks, and employees see their own record.

Can we keep records of former employees?

Yes. When someone leaves, their profile is archived with its full history, so experience letters and audits are easy later.
