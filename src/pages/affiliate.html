Affiliate FAQ

Questions about the program

How much do I earn?

A flat 20% of what the customer you referred pays for the first plan they sign up for. The rate is the same for every referral.

What counts as the first plan term?

The billing period of the plan the customer first buys. On an annual plan, that's the whole first year's payment. On a monthly plan, it's the first month's payment.

Do I earn on renewals?

No. Commission covers the customer's first plan term only, so renewals after that term aren't included.

How do you know a customer came from me?

You get a personal referral link and code. Customers who sign up or book a demo through your link, or quote your code, are recorded as your referrals.

When and how do I get paid?

Commission is paid after the customer has paid for their plan. The payment schedule and method are set out in your affiliate agreement.

What's the difference between affiliates and partners?

Affiliates introduce businesses and earn a commission. Partners go further and set WorkDesk up for their clients. See the partner program if that sounds like you.

Full terms are in the affiliate agreement you receive when your application is approved.
