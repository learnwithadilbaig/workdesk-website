This policy explains how {{cfg:company_legal_name}} ("WorkDesk", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use the WorkDesk HR and payroll software.

Who this policy covers

There are three groups of people whose information we handle:

  • Website visitors who browse this site or fill in a form.
  • Customers and their users: the businesses that subscribe to WorkDesk, and the owners, HR staff and managers who sign in.
  • Employees of our customers, whose records our customers store in WorkDesk. For this information, our customer decides what is collected and why, and we process it on their behalf and on their instructions.

Information we collect

When you visit the website or fill in a form

Details you give us, such as your name, work email, company, phone number, team size and your message. Our web server may also record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site running.

When your company uses WorkDesk

Account details for the people who sign in, and the records your company chooses to store: for example employee profiles, CNIC numbers, contact details, bank details, attendance, leave, salary and payroll records, tasks and performance reviews.

How we use information

  • To respond to demo requests and messages, and to send quotes.
  • To provide, maintain and support the WorkDesk service for our customers.
  • To keep accounts and data secure, and to prevent misuse.
  • To send service messages, such as changes to the product or to these terms.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use our customers' employee data for our own marketing.

Sharing information

We share information only with service providers that help us run WorkDesk, such as hosting, email delivery and form handling, under agreements that require them to protect it, or when the law requires us to. A list of current service providers is available on request.

Fonts and third-party resources

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when you visit. The site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep information

We keep website enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and no longer than [period]. Customer data is kept for the duration of the subscription. After a subscription ends, customers can export their data, and we delete it within [period] unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and regular backups to protect information. See our security page for details. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected customers promptly if we become aware of a breach that affects their data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. If you're an employee of one of our customers, please contact your employer first, since they control your records in WorkDesk. We will help them respond.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top. For significant changes, we'll notify customers by email.
